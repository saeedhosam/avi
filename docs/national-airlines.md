# National Airlines by Country

A country-by-country reference to flag carriers and national airlines.

**Last reviewed:** November 2026

A **flag carrier** is an airline that represents its country internationally and often enjoys preferential rights from its government. It does not have to be state-owned. Some countries have more than one flag carrier, and some have none at all.

## How to read this list

- **National airline** means the airline generally regarded as the country's flag carrier, not every airline based there.
- **State-owned** generally means majority government ownership; minority stakes are noted where relevant.
- **None** means the country currently has no designated flag carrier. Defunct predecessors and planned replacements are noted where useful.
- Ownership and operating status change frequently. Several carriers below operate limited, seasonal, or charter-only services.

## Sources and caveats

This list was compiled from public flag-carrier and airline references, including Wikipedia's [Flag carrier](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flag_carrier) article and individual airline profiles. It covers the 193 UN member states plus Palestine, Vatican City, Taiwan, Kosovo, and selected territories.

Airline ownership, routes, and operating status change often — especially in markets affected by conflict, sanctions, or restructuring. Confirm current service and designation with the airline or the relevant civil aviation authority before relying on this list for operational decisions.
